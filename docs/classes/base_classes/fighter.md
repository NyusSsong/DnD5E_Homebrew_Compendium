# The Fighter

A master of martial combat, the Fighter is a versatile warrior skilled with a variety of weapons and armor. Fighters learn to be deadly with weapons, to withstand the fiercest attacks, and to develop unique techniques to overcome any foe. Whether a noble knight protecting the weak, a disciplined soldier leading armies, or a wandering mercenary seeking fortune, the Fighter's path is one of martial excellence and battlefield dominance.
