# The Blood Hunter

Blood Hunters are warriors who have undergone a dark alchemical ritual known as the Hunter's Bane, suffusing their blood with sinister magic to combat the monsters that prey upon the innocent. They harness ancient Blood Rites, sacrifice their own vitality to fuel deadly effects, and join secretive Occult Orders that shape their methods of monster-slaying.
