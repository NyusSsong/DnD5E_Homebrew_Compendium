# The Shaman

Since time immemorial, Shamans have served as the spiritual intermediaries between the mortal and spirit worlds. Wandering the wilds, or dwelling in the heart of small communities, these spiritual leaders channel the power of primal Spirits to work wonders. Unlike their contemporaries who call on powers from other planes, or channel energy from within, Shamans derive their power from the Spirits present in all living things.

Shamans bind primal Spirits to sacred Totems, channeling their collective power to wield great spiritual magic. The more Totems they carry, the more versatile and powerful a Shaman becomes. At the heart of their power is the art of Totemic Assault, the ability to set Spirits loose upon their foes and unleash spiritual destruction.
