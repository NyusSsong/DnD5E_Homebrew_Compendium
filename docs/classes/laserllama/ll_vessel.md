# The Vessel

A lucky few mortals are called Vessels — their souls chosen as prisons for great and powerful entities sealed away by the gods themselves. These Spirits, for better or worse, grant their hosts abilities beyond those of normal folk. A Vessel must learn to control the power of the entity bound to their soul, lest they lose themselves to the Spirit within.
