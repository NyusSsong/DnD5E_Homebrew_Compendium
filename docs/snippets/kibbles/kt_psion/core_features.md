#### Hit Points

**Hit Dice:** 1d6 per Psion level  
**Hit Points at 1st Level:** 6 + your Constitution modifier  
**Hit Points at Higher Levels:** 1d6 (or 4) + your Constitution modifier per Psion level after 1st  

<hr>

#### Proficiencies  

**Armor:** Light armor  
**Weapons:** Simple weapons  
**Tools:** None  

**Saving Throws:** Intelligence, Wisdom  
**Skills:** Psionics, and choose two from Deception, History, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation, Medicine, Perception, or Religion  

<hr>

#### Equipment

* *(a)* a quarterstaff, *(b)* a dagger, or *(c)* a martial weapon (if proficient)  
* *(a)* leather armor or *(b)* scale mail (if proficient)  
* *(a)* a scholar’s pack or *(b)* an explorer’s pack  

<hr>

#### Level 1 - Psionic Archetype

At 1st level, you choose the archetype of Psion you embody, selecting from Awakened Mind, Unleashed Mind, Transcended Mind, Shaper’s Mind, Wandering Mind, or Consuming Mind. Your choice grants you features at 1st level, and again at 3rd, 6th, 10th, and 14th level.

<hr>

#### Level 1 - Psionics

You gain access to a psionic discipline granted by your archetype. Each discipline includes a passive feature and an active psionic power that can be empowered with Psi Points. You can also use your discipline to recreate certain spells, as detailed at the end of each discipline description. At 3rd level you select a second discipline, and at 18th level you select a third.

Psionic powers are suppressed by antimagic fields, can be dispelled with *dispel magic*, and are affected by *counterspell* only when recreating a spell. Detect magic identifies psionic powers as untyped magical energy, not the original spell.

Using a psionic effect to recreate a spell requires no verbal or material components, but does require somatic gestures and causes you to glow with psionic energy.

<hr>

#### Level 1 - Psi Points

Starting at 1st level, you gain Psi Points equal to your Psion level. You regain all spent points after a short or long rest. You can spend up to half your Psion level (rounded up) on a single psionic effect. For example, a 5th-level Psion can spend up to 3 Psi Points at once.

<hr>

#### Level 1 - Psionic Ability

Your psionic powers, Psionic Talents, and psion-class spells use your psionic ability:

**Psionic Save DC** = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier

**Psionic Attack Modifier** = your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier

<hr>

#### Level 2 - Psionic Talents

At 2nd level, you select two Psionic Talents from the list at the end of this class description. You gain additional talents at 5th, 7th, 9th, 12th, 15th, and 18th levels. When you level up, you may replace a previously chosen talent with a new one.

<hr>

#### Level 3 - Second Discipline

At 3rd level, you gain a second psionic discipline. You cannot choose a discipline you already know. You gain all features associated with the new discipline.

<hr>

#### Level 4 - Ability Score Improvement

When you reach 4th level, and again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level, you can increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1. You cannot increase an ability above 20 using this feature.

<hr>

#### Level 5 - Psionic Mastery

At 5th level, you gain mastery over your psionic powers. At the start of your turn, you regain 1 free Psi Point that can be spent to empower disciplines (but not talents or spell recreation). At 11th level this increases to 2 free points, and at 17th level to 3 points. Free points may be split between abilities, but are lost at the end of your turn if unused.

<hr>

#### Level 11 - Innate Psionics

At 11th level, you gain one 6th-level innate psionic spell that can be cast once per long rest. At higher levels, you gain additional innate spells: one 7th-level spell at 13th, one 8th-level at 15th, and one 9th-level at 17th. Innate Psionics are not limited by disciplines but require any spell components.

<hr>

#### Level 18 - Third Discipline

At 18th level, you gain a third psionic discipline, gaining all features associated with it. You cannot select a discipline you already know.

<hr>

#### Level 20 - Ascension

At 20th level, when you die, you may become an incorporeal entity on the Border Ethereal at your death location in the Material Plane. This form has the physical abilities (Strength, Dexterity, Constitution) and ghostly abilities of a ghost, but retains your mental ability scores and Psion features. Your current and maximum hit points match the ghost form. If you have fewer than 10 Psi Points when assuming this form, you have 10. You cannot regain spent abilities while in this form, and if you exhaust all Psi Points or the ghost is destroyed, you die normally.
