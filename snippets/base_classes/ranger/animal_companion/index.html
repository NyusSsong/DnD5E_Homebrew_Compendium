    Animal Companion

    Level 3 - Young Animal Companion

    The stat block of your animal companion is the same as the beast, but it has the following changes:

    • At 1st level, you have a Young Animal Companion. As you gain levels in the Ranger class, your animal companion improves. Additionaly, when you get your animal companion, one of its abilities increases by +2.
    • Your animal companion does not gain the Multiattack action.
    • Your animal companion has a number of hit dice equal to your Ranger level, which are d10s, and it adds its Constitution modifier to each of them to calculate its hit points. Your animal companion can spend these dice as part of a short rest as usual.
    • Unless it is already proficient, your animal companion gains proficiency in Perception, Survival, Acrobatics, Athletics and all saving throws. Unless it has an Intelligence score of 8 or higher, it cannot perform tasks that require of a higher intelligence.
    • As long as you and your animal companion can see each other, you can establish non-verbal communication.

    Additionally, you learn the Wild Insight I Knack, but it doesn't count against your Knacks Known. If you already know it, you learn another Knack.

    Level 6 - Mature Animal Companion

    At 6th level, your animal companion becomes a Mature Animal companion. It gains the following benefits:

    • Its Strength, Dexterity, Constitution and Wisdom scores increase by +1.
    • It gains an extra damage dice on its attacks.
    • It gains the Extra Attack action.
    • It gains expertise on Perception and becomes proficient in Intimidation and Stealth.
    • If its size is Medium or lower, it increases by one step.
    • Its attacks are considered magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.

    Additionally, your animal companion gains all benefits of your Ranger's Quarry, including any Knacks that enhance it.

    Level 10 - Incredible Animal Companion

    At 10th level, your animal companion continues growing and becomes an Incredible Animal Companion. You must choose one of the following subtypes, gaining their benefits:

    • Elemental Companion. The nature of the magic that surrounds your animal companion becomes elemental magic instead of primal. Its Wisdom score increases by +2 and its Strength, Dexterity and Constitution increase by +1. Additionally, you must choose the elemental subtype linked to your animal companion: djinn (lightning), efreet (fire), marid (cold) and dao (acid). The damage of your animal companion changes according to the elemental subtype. Your animal companion also gains resistance to the damage of the elemental subtype selected.
    • Indomitable Companion. Your animal companion becomes incredibly resilient. Its Constitution score increases by +2 and its Strength, Dexterity and Wisdom scores increase by +1. Its HD increase by one step and become d12. It gains an extra damage dice. Additionally, if its size is Medium or lower, it increases by one step. Your animal companion also gains a bonus to its AC equal to half your Wisdom modifier (rounded up).
    • Nimble Companion. Your animal companion becomes extremely agile and quick. Its Dexterity score increases by +2 and its Strength Constitution and Wisdom scores increase by +1. It gains an extra damage dice. Additionally, its movement speed increases by +10 and a +1 bonus to its AC. Your animal companion also gains the Evasion feature.
    • Wild Companion. Your animal companion becomes fierce and ferocious. Its Strength score increases by +2 and its Dexterity, Constitution and Wisdom scores increase by +1. It gains two extra damage dice. Additionally, if its size is Medium or lower, it increases by one step. Your animal companion also gains a bonus to its damage equal to half your Wisdom modifier (rounded up).

    Level 14 - Specialized Animal Companion

    At 14th level, the Intelligence of your animal companion improves and becomes a Specialized Animal Companion. Its Dexterity score increases by +2 and its Intelligence score increases by +4. It gains an extra damage dice. Additionally, you must choose one of the following specializations, gaining their benefits:

    • Ambusher. In its natural environment, your animal companion feels extremely comfortable. It gains expertise on Stealth and its Dexterity score increases by +1.
    • Bully. Your animal companion terrorizes foes with dominance displays and pushes them around the battlefield. It gains expertise on Athletics and Intimidation. Its Strength score increases by +1 and its Charisma score increases by +3.
    • Daredevil. Your animal companion joins the fray with graceful leaps and dives. It gains immunity to being flanked. It gains expertise on Acrobatics. Its Dexterity score increases by +1.
    • Deep Diver (prerequisite: Elemental Companion [Marid]). The cold energy of your animal companion grows stronger. It gains immunity to cold damage. It gains a swim speed of 30 feet or its swim speed increases by 30 feet if it already had one. It gains expertise on Athletics. Its Constitution score increases by +1.
    • Racer. Your animal companion becomes extremely fast. All the speeds of your animal companion increase by 10 feet. It gains expertise on Athletics. Its Constitution score increases by +1.
    • Steadfast Strider (prerequisite: Elemental Companion [Dao]). The earth energy of your animal companion grows stronger. It gains immunity to acid damage. It ignores difficult terrain and can move through quicksand, clay or similar elements as if it were solid ground. It gains expertise on Athletics. Its Strength score increases by +1.
    • Tracker. Your companion is an incredible tracker. It gains expertise on Survival. Its Wisdom score increases by +1.
    • Wildfire Scorcher (prerequisite: Elemental Companion [Efreet]). The fire energy of your animal companion grows stronger. It gains immunity to fire damage. Whenever a creature grapples your animal companion, is grappled by it or hits it with a melee attack, the creature takes 2d6 fire damage. Its Dexterity score increases by +1.
    • Wind Chaser (prerequisite: Elemental Companion [Djinn]). The wind energy of your animal companion grows stronger. It gains immunity to lightning damage. Its movement speed increases by 20 feet and it gains a flight speed of 20 feet. If it already has one, it increases by 20 feet. It gains expertise on Acrobatics. Its Dexterity score increases by +1.
    • Wrecker. Your companion smashes things. Its attacks deal 4d6 additional damages to objects. It gains expertise on Athletics. Its Strength score increases by +1.

    Additionally, the bond with your animal companion reaches its apex. When you cast a Ranger spell that targets yourself, the your animal companion also gains the benefits as long as it is within 30 feet of you.
