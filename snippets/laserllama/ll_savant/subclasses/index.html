    Choose an academic discipline:

    ??? subclass "Archaeologist" Specializing in the study of lost civilizations, ancient ruins, and uncharted lands, Archaeologists bring the light of discovery to the dark and deadly places of the world. Doing their best to uncover the wisdom of ages past, they tend to feel kinship with the civilizations they study and go to great lengths not to offend their memory.
    ### Level 3 - Student of Archaeology You gain proficiency in both History and Investigation. If you are already proficient in History or Investigation, you instead add your Intellect Die to any ability check you make with that skill. Time spent exploring ancient places has given you a knack for navigating their dangers, granting you the following benefits: - You learn to speak, read, and write two extra languages of your choice, often archaic or forgotten tongues. - Any time you make an ability check related to a trap, you add your Intellect Die to the result of your roll. - If you spend at least 1 minute examining an object, you can ascertain its value, origin, and age. If it has magical properties, you learn them as if you had cast *identify*.
    ### Level 3 - Eye for Antiquity Your knowledge of ancient civilizations allows you to unlock lost secrets of their technology. When you spend a long rest in a Settlement or dungeon, you can spend 1 hour examining antiques and debris to unearth one Curio of your choice. The Curio is a Tiny magical item with the properties of one Common magical item of your choice. If the Curio requires a Spellcasting Ability, you use your Intelligence for it. Due to advanced age and fragility, you must spend 1 hour during each long rest maintaining your Curios, or they revert into mundane objects. You can maintain a number of Curios equal to your Intelligence modifier (a minimum of one).
    ### Level 7 - Adventuring Academic Your ability to navigate deadly ruins in search of knowledge is without peer. Whenever you make a saving throw to avoid the effects of a trap, you add your Intellect Die to your roll. You also gain a climb speed equal to your walking speed. ??? info "Ancient Artifacts" Eye for Antiquity is a rare ability that gives access to magical items of your choice. Discuss with your DM to determine items appropriate to your game.
    ### Level 7 - Ancient Insights You have advanced in your understanding of ancient technologies. You ignore all class, race, and alignment restrictions for the attunement and use of magic items, scrolls, and potions. If such a magical item, scroll, or potion produces a spell that requires concentration, you can maintain concentration on the spell and your Focus at the same time. However, if you are forced to make a saving throw to maintain concentration, you make a single saving throw for both effects.
    ### Level 13 - Lore Master You study every detail of every myth, legend, and folk tale you come across. When you observe a person, place, or object for at least 10 minutes, you mystically recall information about it as if it were the target of a *legend lore* spell. The target of this feature does not need to be of legendary importance in order for you to gain information. However, if there is no relevant lore about the target, you learn nothing.
    ### Level 13 - Unearthed Arcana You are an unparalleled expert with ancient magical devices. When you use Eye for Antiquity, you can unearth Curios with the properties of Common or Uncommon magic items. You can also use your Intellect Save DC for your magic item saving throws unless the magic item's innate DC is higher.
    ### Level 18 - Master Archaeologist Exposure to the magic of the ancient world has given you an innate resilience to its effects. You gain Resistance to damage from spells, magic items, and magical traps. Finally, you can have a single Curio with the properties of a Rare magic item.
    ??? subclass "Investigator" Masters at unraveling mysteries, conspiracies, and secrets of all kinds, Investigators possess an uncanny ability to read the intent of others. They often dedicate their lives to thwarting those who deceive the innocent and take advantage of the common trust. Their considerable intellect and eye for the truth stand in the way of thieves, shapeshifters, and corrupt politicians.
    ### Level 3 - Student of Truth You gain proficiency in both Insight and Investigation. If you are already proficient in either Insight or Investigation, you instead add your Intellect Die to checks with that skill. Your intuitive nature also grants you the following benefits: - You can use your Intelligence, in place of Wisdom, for all Insight and Perception checks, including passive checks. - When you take the Search action, you gain information as if you spent a full minute searching instead of one action. - If you spend 1 minute speaking with your Focus, you can learn one of its Ideals, Traits, Bonds, or Flaws.
    ### Level 3 - Rough & Tumble You have gained some unsavory skills while fighting shadows in the underbelly of civilization. You learn to speak, read, and decode Thieves' Cant, the secret language of criminals. Also, your unarmed strikes deal Bludgeoning damage equal to your Intellect Die + your Strength modifier on hit. Once per turn when you hit your Focus with a melee attack you can force it to make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, you choose if it is Blinded, Deafened, or unable to speak until the start of your next turn. Or, if your Focus is Large or smaller, you can choose for it to be knocked Prone.
    ### Level 7 - Illicit Contacts You are deeply familiar with the criminal elements of society. While communicating in Thieves' Cant, you add your Intellect Die to any ability checks you make to influence anyone who understands Thieves' Cant. You also master the Secrets & Whispers Scholarly Pursuit, but it does not count against your number of Pursuits. If you already mastered Secrets & Whispers, you instead master either Perfect Recall or Traditions.
    ### Level 7 - Underhanded Brawler You learned to fight in back alleys, where the only thing that matters is who is left standing at the end. You gain the following underhanded benefits: **Cunning Flourish.** If you use Calculated Flourish and the attack misses, you can force your attacker to make a Dexterity saving throw against your Rough & Tumble feature as part of the same reaction. **Discombobulate.** If your Focus fails its saving throw against Rough & Tumble, you can apply two of the normal effects instead of one. You can also forgo one of the effects to instead cause it to take Bludgeoning damage equal to your Intellect Die. **Underhanded Strike.** Whenever you take the Attack action on your turn, you can make a single unarmed strike as a bonus action.
    ### Level 13 - Piercing Gaze You see through the most intricate deception and conspiracy. You are always aware if your Focus is lying, and you instantly detect the presence of visual Illusions and Shapeshifters that are within your line of sight. However, this does not allow you to see through either of those effects. In addition, when your Focus fails its saving throw against Rough & Tumble, you can choose for it to be Stunned for the duration, in place of the normal Rough & Tumble effects.
    ### Level 18 - Master Investigator Your sense for the truth rivals immortals and myths. You gain Truesight out to a 30-foot radius. Within this radius, you also automatically detect hidden doors and traps. Last, when you hit your Focus with an attack or use Potent Observation against your Focus, you can cause that attack to become an automatic critical hit. Once you do so, you must finish a short or long rest before you can do so again.
    ??? feature "Mentor" While most Savants use their vast intellect in pursuit of their chosen discipline, Mentors use their gifts in service of others. Most often, they are past their prime and looking to pass their knowledge to the next generation. Whether community elder, retired adventurer, or someone with knowledge beyond their years, Mentors are ready to gently guide those around them.
    ### Level 3 - Student of Life You gain proficiency in both History and Insight. If you are already proficient in History or Insight, you instead add your Intellect Die to ability checks with that skill. Your knowledge of life also grants you the benefits below: - You master the Instruction Scholarly Pursuit. If you have mastered this Pursuit, you instead master Quick Study. - When you use Potent Observation and you roll a 1, you can re-roll your die, but you must use your new roll. - You can take the Help action as a bonus action.
    ### Level 3 - Astute Advice You know just what to say to help others learn from failures. When another creature within 30 feet that can both hear and understand you fails an ability check, attack roll, or a saving throw, you can use a reaction to have it re-roll its d20. You can use this reaction a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of once). You regain one use when you finish a short rest, and all uses after a long rest.
    ### Level 7 - Adept Assistance You are always ready with quick words of advice. When you take the Help action, you can target two creatures. Moreover, you can use the Help action to aid a creature within 5 feet of you in attacking your Focus.
    ### Level 7 - Soothing Presence Your calming presence allows others to truly relax. Creatures that complete a short rest with you have advantage on any Hit Die rolls they make to regain their hit points. They also gain a number of temporary hit points equal to your Savant level.
    ### Level 13 - Wondrous Advice Your insightful observations carry your allies to new heights. When you use Astute Advice, the creature adds your Intellect Die to the new result of its roll. You also regain all uses of this feature each time you finish a short or long rest.
    ### Level 13 - Mystical Intuition Your life experience and extraordinary intuition can give you mystical insights into the world around you. You can spend 1 minute meditating on a question you have, or a question that another creature has asked you. You then mystically intuit the answer to the question as if you had cast the *commune* spell. However, unlike *commune*, you can only intuit an answer if it is known to another mortal. Once you gain an answer to a question using this feature, you must finish a long rest before you can do so again.
    ### Level 18 - Master Mentor Your advice is legendary and desired throughout the land, as you always know what to say. You gain the following benefits: - Other creatures of your choice within 15 feet can add your Intelligence modifier (minimum of +1) to all ability checks and saving throws, so long as they can hear you. - When you target a creature with the Help action, it gains temporary hit points equal to your Intelligence modifier. - You can use Mystical Intuition once per short or long rest.
    ??? subclass "Naturalist" The Naturalist's classroom begins at the edges of civilization. Scholars of the wild, they will go to great lengths to preserve nature as it exists, free from civilization. A true Naturalist is both conservationist and expert at predicting weather, caring for wild animals, identifying toxic and medicinal plants, and guiding others safely through uncharted wilds of the world.
    ### Level 3 - Student of Nature You gain proficiency in both Animal Handling and Nature. If already proficient in Animal Handling or Nature, you instead add your Intellect Die to ability checks with that skill. Your knowledge of the wild also grants you these benefits: - You can use your Intelligence, instead of your Wisdom, for any Animal Handling or Survival checks you make. - You can mark a creature as your Focus by analyzing signs of its passing, such as tracks, even if you cannot see it.
    ### Level 3 - Naturalist's Journal You compile your research on fantastical flora and fauna in a Naturalist's Journal. During the course of a short or long rest, you can spend 1 hour detailing your current Environment, or a specific Beast, Plant, or Monstrosity that was your Focus within the past 24 hours. You gain these benefits against creatures and Environments in your Journal: - You have advantage on related Intelligence checks. - Once per turn when you make an attack roll against a detailed creature, you add your Intellect Die to your attack roll. - In a detailed Environment, you and those travelling with you can ignore non-magical difficult terrain, and you cannot get lost.
    ### Level 7 - Call of the Wild Drawing upon eons of understanding, you can bend wild creatures to your will. As an action, you can force a creature within 30 feet that is also detailed in your Journal to make a Charisma saving throw. Creatures of a CR equal to half your Savant level or higher automatically succeed. On a failure, it is Charmed by you and uses these rules for the duration: **Control.** The creature is Friendly to you and your allies. As a bonus action, you can issue a verbal command, which it will do its best to obey on its next turn. When it is not carrying out a command, it will defend itself to the best of its abilities. **Morale.** If the creature takes damage, it repeats its saving throw at the start of its next turn. On a success, it is no longer Charmed. If it makes this saving throw, you can use a reaction to subtract one Intellect Die from its roll if it can hear you. **Duration.** If the creature does not escape sooner, the effect ends if you become Unconscious, you free it with a command, or you attempt to use this feature on another creature. Once a creature succeeds on any saving throw against this feature, it is immune to its effects until it finishes a long rest.
    ### Level 13 - Advanced Studies Your knowledge of the wild does not stop with the mundane. You can add notes on Dragons, Giants, Oozes, and Undead to your Journal. Your Journal also grants additional benefits: - You gain Focus benefits against the detailed creatures. - While in a detailed Environment, you and those travelling with you ignore magical difficult terrain and make saving throws to resist environmental effects with advantage.
    ### Level 18 - Master Naturalist Your natural knowledge surpasses all others. You can add any non-Humanoid creature to your Journal, and you have advantage on attack rolls against creatures detailed in your Journal. Finally, Call of the Wild only ends when you willingly free the creature, you use it on another creature, or you or the Charmed creature dies.
    ??? subclass "Physician" Physicians use their considerable intellect to heal the sick and tend to the wounded. They spend their lives studying the anatomy and biology of mortals, and use this knowledge to keep their allies in top condition and cripple foes. Using this medical training, they offer aid to those that do not have access to the luxury of divine magic or other healing spells.
    ### Level 3 - Student of Medicine You gain proficiency in Medicine and Sleight of Hand. If you are already proficient in Medicine or Sleight of Hand, you instead add your Intellect Die to ability checks with that skill. Your studies also grant you the benefits below: - You can use Intelligence, in place of Wisdom, for any Medicine checks you make. - For each minute you spend examining your Focus, you can identify a disease, poison, or curse currently afflicting it. - When you deal Slashing damage to your Focus, you can reduce its speed by 10 feet until the beginning of your next turn.
    ### Level 3 - Combat Medic You have studied to administer medicinal aid in the field. During a short or long rest, you can spend 1 hour with a Healer's Kit to restore a number of its uses equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of 1). You also gain the actions below, which you can only use on other creatures. When you use one of these actions, you can expend one use of a Healer's Kit you are holding to treat any Intellect Dice rolled as the maximum possible result: **Adrenaline Jolt.** You touch a creature that has a disease, or is Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, or Poisoned, and it can immediately repeat its saving throw to end that effect with a bonus equal to your Intellect Die. **Dress Wounds.** You touch a creature that is below its hit point maximum, and it gains temporary hit points equal to your Intellect Die. These temporary hit points can't exceed the number of hit points the creature is missing. **Healing Surge.** One creature you touch can immediately expend one of its Hit Dice to regain hit points equal to that Hit Die + its Constitution modifier + your Intellect Die. If you use this ability on a living creature at 0 hit points, it is instantly Stabilized even if it does not expend a Hit Die.
    ### Level 7 - Field Doctor You easily administer aid in the chaos of battle. After you use a Combat Medic action, you can use your bonus action to take the Dash or Disengage action, or make one weapon attack.
    ### Level 7 - Steady Hands Your confidence in your medical capabilities has grown. You can use Combat Medic actions on yourself, so long as you are not Blinded, Incapacitated, Paralyzed, or Restrained.
    ### Level 13 - Medical Expertise Your techniques push allies to their physical limit. When you use a Combat Medic action, you can empower it, and it gains the bonuses detailed below. You can do so a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (a minimum of once), and you regain all uses when you finish a short or long rest: **Adrenaline Jolt.** You instantly end one of these conditions: Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Stunned, one level of Exhaustion, one reduction to an ability score, or one reduction to its maximum hit points. **Dress Wounds.** You can either reattach one severed limb or digit, or grant the target temporary hit points equal to the difference between its current and maximum hit points. **Healing Surge.** You can use this feature on a creature that has died within the past minute, and it can spend one Hit Die to return to life with hit points from your Healing Surge. This feature has no effect on a creature that died of old age, and it cannot restore missing body parts.
    ### Level 18 - Master Physician Your knowledge of anatomy allows you to perform legendary feats of medicine. When a creature you are touching expends a Hit Die to regain hit points, it treats it as the maximum roll. This includes any Hit Dice spent during short rests, and Hit Dice spent as part of your Healing Surge action.
    ??? subclass "Tactician" Every successful monarch, conqueror, and revolutionary has a master Tactician responsible for their victory. These shrewd leaders remain always one step ahead of foes and have a plan for every outcome. Alone, a Tactician is not a threat, but with powerful allies, they become a formidable fighting force.
    ### Level 3 - Student of War You gain proficiency in both History and Persuasion. If you are already proficient in History or Persuasion, you instead add your Intellect Die to ability checks with that skill. Your study of warfare also grants you the benefits below: - You gain proficiency in two Gaming Sets of your choice, and whenever you make an ability check that uses any Gaming Set, add your Intellect Die to the roll. - You gain proficiency in medium armor, shields, and all martial weapons that do not have the Heavy property.
    ### Level 3 - Tactical Command You can use your knowledge of tactics to direct your allies on the battlefield. You learn to use the following Orders. To issue an Order, take the Attack action, then forgo an attack to issue an Order to another creature that can see or hear you within 30 feet. You can forgo any number of attacks to issue Orders: **Attack Order.** If this creature takes the Attack action before the beginning of your next turn, it makes one additional attack as part of that Attack action. **Defensive Order.** The creature gains the benefits of the Dodge action until the start of your next turn. **Maneuvering Order.** As a reaction, this creature can move up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks. **Support Order.** This creature can take the Help, Hide, Search, or Use an Object action.
    ### Level 7 - Advanced Tactics Your continued study of the art of war grants you knowledge of the following additional Orders, which use the same rules: **Enlivening Order.** On the creature's next turn, it gains the benefits of the Dash action, and it has advantage on Strength and Dexterity checks. **Rejuvenating Order.** The creature can immediately repeat one saving throw to end one condition currently affecting it.
    ### Level 7 - Strategic Superiority You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. Moreover, if you use your action to Dash, Dodge, or Disengage, you can make a single attack or issue an Order as a bonus action on that turn.
    ### Level 13 - Tactical Genius Your genius allows you to control the flow of each battle from the outset. When you roll Initiative, you can issue one Order before any creatures have a chance to act. Also, when another creature that can hear you attacks your Focus, you can use Potent Observation on its attack roll. You can do so after it rolls, but before you know if its attack hits.
    ### Level 18 - Master Tactician Your words inspire heroism in your allies. Any time you issue an Order to a creature, you can grant it temporary hit points equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of 1). Finally, you learn the following legendary Orders. These Orders can only be issued once each per short or long rest: **Heroic Order.** Until the beginning of your next turn, the creature has Resistance to all damage, and it has advantage on all ability checks, attack rolls, and saving throws. **Revitalizing Order.** You issue this Order to a creature that died in the past minute. It immediately stands up and regains hit points equal to your level + your Intelligence modifier. This Order cannot revive creatures that die of old age.
